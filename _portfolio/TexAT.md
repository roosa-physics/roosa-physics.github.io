Developed by the Rogachev group at the Texas A&M University Cyclotron Institute, the [TexAT-TPC](https://cyclotron.tamu.edu/facilities/texat-tpc/) or the Texas Actove Target - Time Projection Chamber is an experimental end station designed for nuclear structure and reaction studies.   
As the name suggests, it is an active target system meaning that the dection medium in TexAT studies doubles as the reaction target. 
This feature was a key aspect of my PhD research under the direction of [Gregory Christian](https://www.ap.smu.ca/~gchristian/index.html) and [Grigory Rogachev](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1120-6103), which focused on developing zero straggling charged particle spectroscopy measurements of light isotopes.
