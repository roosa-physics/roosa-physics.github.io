Developed the [Christopher Wrede Group](https://people.frib.msu.edu/~wrede/) at [FRIB](https://frib.msu.edu/), is a detection system designed to measure $\beta$-delayed proton emmision processes relevent to astrophysical contexts. 
I joined the group as an undergraduate researcher and made contributions to their early simulation tool kits.
