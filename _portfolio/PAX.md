Under the direction of [Nancy Paul](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4668-8958) and [Paul Indelicato](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4668-8958) in the exotic ions group at [Laboratoire Kaslter Brossel](https://www.lkb.fr/), I have been working on the Antiprotonic Atom X-Ray Spectroscopy (PAX) project. 
We push forward the state-of-the-art in hard-x-ray spectroscopy of exotic atoms by deploying superconducting transition edge sensors developed by the [NIST Quantum Sensors Division](https://www.nist.gov/pml/quantum-sensors) at the [CERN Antimatter Factory](https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter/).
